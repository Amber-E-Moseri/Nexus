## What to Expect

This Is It isn't just another program. It's tailored for you, and it requires something from you: expectation.

Scripture tells us, *"The earnest expectation of the righteous shall not be cut short."* You must come with distinct expectations. What specific changes do you want to see? In your walk with him? In the work?

Yes, you'll see friends from across the region. But remember: you're here to receive something — to *lambano*. *"One word from God can change your life forever."* Are you ready to receive those words?

Prepare your spirit. Join us in times of prayer and participate in the fast as you ready yourself. Come expectant. Come ready to testify that This Is It was the moment everything changed.

### 🙏 Prayer & Fasting (Aug 24–27)

As we count down to This Is It, we're setting aside time to prepare ourselves. Join us as we take time to pray and fast Monday through Thursday (Aug 24–27).
