Landing in Winnipeg

Getting There

🛬 Airport arrival

Winnipeg James Armstrong Richardson International Airport (YWG). This is the airport to fly into. It's only about 10 minutes from the hotel.

Head to arrivals and look out for the Sandman shuttle — they'll have your name on their list.

🚐 Hotel shuttle

Good to know

The airport is close by, and a driver from the hotel shuttle will come get you. To ensure your name is on their pickup list, fill out the flight form above with your arrival details. Just head to arrivals and look out for the Sandman shuttle.

🕐 Your arrival time

Your arrival time depends on your department. Check with your department lead for your specific arrival window so you can coordinate with the shuttle.

Not in a department? Target checking in by 4:30 PM at the latest so you'll be ready for the opening session at 6:00 PM.
